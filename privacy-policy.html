3. Categories of Data Collected & Purposes of Processing

Easy Bode is primarily a client-side engineering analysis tool. Most calculations (transfer functions, Bode, Nyquist, Nichols diagrams, temporal responses) are performed locally within your browser using JavaScript libraries (MathJS, Chart.js, MathJax). However, certain technical and storage interactions occur:

3.1. Automatically Collected Technical Data (Hosting Provider)

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc.). When you access the site, GitHub automatically collects standard server logs, which may include:

  • IP address (often truncated or anonymized by infrastructure providers)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages visited and referring URLs

Legal Basis: Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to ensure website security, operational stability, and protection against malicious traffic or DDoS attacks.

3.2. Local Storage Data (Client-Side Preferences)

To enhance user experience, Easy Bode utilizes your browser's localStorage mechanism to save:

  • Function calculation history (recently analyzed transfer functions)
  • User interface preferences (Dark/Light mode, selected measurement units like Hz or rad/s)
  • Chart zoom, pan, and layer visibility states

Note: This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to us or third parties unless explicitly exported by you (e.g., via PDF download). You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you possess robust rights regarding your personal data under Chapter III of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request confirmation whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of your personal data where applicable.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Limit how your data is used under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing (e.g., advertising cookies).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@easybode.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority (e.g., Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy).
